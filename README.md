# Hi everyone, I'm Roy

I'm a data scientist and machine learning engineer. I studied electronics engineering, spent 5+ years building safety-critical embedded software, and I'm finishing an MS in Data Science at ITESO.

I've always been drawn to mathematics, physics, and philosophy, and really to any field where there is something to understand. I'm curious by nature: I like taking ideas apart, finding where they come from, and going deep when time allows.

That curiosity is why I changed careers. You only get one professional life, so beyond the salary, I want my work to match what I care about. When it does, you can give it your full time knowing none of it is wasted, and that is when you do your best work.

**What I'm into:** data science, machine learning, deep learning, robotics, and anything where data, computation, and mathematics meet.

**Outside of work:** art, and above all music. If work didn't exist and I could choose one thing to do, it would be music. Like Schopenhauer, I see it as the highest of the arts, and maybe the best thing that has ever happened to humanity.
